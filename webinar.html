What you’ll learn

After the webinar, you will know how to:

  • Deploy AI agents for your work — no prompts, integrations, or tech skills needed.
  • Optimize I/E criteria in advance for smoother enrollment.
  • Build from scratch data-driven, month-by-month forecasts in <5 minutes.
  • Predict trial completion times at any point, before or during the trial.
  • Simulate and compare scenarios based on their ROI.
  • Preview the impact of changes in real time — for example:
    • Add, remove, or troubleshoot sites.
    • Test eligibility and protocol amendments.
    • Start or pause recruitment campaigns.
  • Get trial-specific suggestions to prevent or correct delays.
  • Use the digital twin of your trial to assess CRO and vendors’ projections.
