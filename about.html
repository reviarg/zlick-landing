About Zlick

Enrollment forecasting should understand the work behind enrollment.

Zlick is built for teams that need a more realistic view of how recruitment will unfold, and what they can do about it.

Why we are building it

Clinical trial enrollment is shaped by more than planned site count and historical averages. Protocol design, eligibility, country mix, seasonality, site experience, competition, patient access, operational delays, and recruitment strategy all interact over time.

Zlick brings those inputs into one structured forecasting workflow so clinical teams can test decisions before timelines slip.

Who it is for

Zlick is designed for clinical operations, feasibility, patient recruitment, innovation, CRO, and executive teams that need to understand enrollment risk early and revisit assumptions as trials evolve.

The goal is practical: clearer forecasts, better scenarios, and better conversations about what to do next.

Principles

Built to make forecasting more explainable, contextual, and useful.

01

Context first

Forecasts should start with the trial, the market, the patients, and the operational plan.

02

Scenario ready

Teams should be able to model real decisions, not just view a static timeline.

03

Explainable

Every forecast should help users understand the drivers, tradeoffs, and limits.

04

Continuously refined

Aggregated feedback can help sharpen future assumptions as the platform learns.

Zlick helps clinical teams forecast enrollment, simulate recruitment decisions, and understand the drivers behind trial timelines.
