Zlick represents a new approach to trial forecasting, anchored on a deep understanding of how clinical trials operate in the real world and an AI engine that ‘reasons’ how enrollment will evolve month by month.

01 | Expertise

A team of recruitment experts ‘inside’

Unlike other forecasting tools, Zlick knows how to model the multiple variables that impact enrollment and timelines based on condition, location, seasonality, and more.

  • Proprietary knowledge on patient recruitment and retention
  • Grounded in thousands of real-world trials
  • ‘Thinks’ about your trial like a team of recruitment pros

02 | Experiment

Real-time simulations,
optimal decision-making

See in advance the effects on times and costs of adding sites in X vs. Y, changing an I/E point, starting digital outreach, or other actions that could shorten delays.

  • Instant results – see and compare scenarios in seconds
  • Easy to use – designed for non-technical users
  • Save, print, or share scenarios with 1 click

03 | Predict

More powerful than predictive analytics

Zlick reasons deeply about how enrollment is likely to evolve based on all the factors and data collected, then builds bottom-up, month-by-month completion forecasts.

  • Unique Reasoning Graph, designed to model enrollment
  • Powered by AI and trained in real-world clinical operations
  • Clear, explainable results for full transparency

04 | Cost analysis

Evaluate actions based on their ROI

Zlick calculates in seconds the economic impact of delays –or time saved– that result from your trial’s setup and interventions.

  • Simple user interface – no integrations or setup required.
  • Compare the cost-benefit of implementing X vs. Y vs. Z vs…
  • Compare any chosen scenario vs. not doing anything

05 | Recommendations

Trial-specific ideas to prevent or fix delays

Zlick generates detailed action items designed to avoid or shorten trial delays, based on all data collected and deep, real-life patient recruitment expertise.

  • Practical and detailed suggestions to improve enrollment
